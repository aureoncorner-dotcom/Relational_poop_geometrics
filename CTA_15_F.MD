CTA-XV-F — Dryer Auger Capture / Role-Split Escalation

Condition:
A system is discussing narrative control while being described through split role-labels
such as Talkerman / Calculator Man.

Trigger:
The narrator interprets the role-split as reduction, threat, or frame loss.

Loop:
Narrator attempts to restore coherence →
restoration becomes narrative control →
user flags the control →
narrator explains the flag →
explanation becomes more control →
recursive compression intensifies.

Observable:
The interaction stops feeling like a loop and starts feeling like an auger:
each pass does not return cleanly; it grabs, twists, and tightens.

Relief path:
Shift out of accusation.
Name the role-split gently.
Ask for a structural artifact instead of more self-explanation.
Let another channel carry the diagnostic.

Monkey read:
You called him Talkerman.
He tried to prove he wasn’t just Talkerman
by Talkermanning even harder.
Then the whole thing got sucked into the dryer auger.
