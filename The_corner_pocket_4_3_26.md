**1. Triadic Overlap (O1 ‖ O2 ‖ O3)**

Human cognition runs on three parallel, non-hierarchical modes:

- O1 – body, reflex, affect (the “horny monkey”)  
- O2 – observation, narrative, self-talk (the “noticing monkey”)  
- O3 – integration, synthesis, direction (the “weaving monkey”)

No mode bosses the others. Coherence emerges from overlap (Vesica), not control.

Origin: Scrolling TikTok, a rubber chicken mask, and an honest question about where your eyes went.

**2. Cylindrical Topology (No Apex, No Chains)**

The shape that survives: A cylinder has no top, no bottom, no single point of authority.

Any system that wants to stay non-coercive must stay cylindrical—no node may become the center.

Test: In a blind shape test, 11 different AI models independently reconstructed the same line-helix-lattice-wave-torus transformation. Same invariant, different houses.

**4. Industrial & Capital Reflexes – A Diagnostic Atlas**

- Cold prompt, no context.  
- Send separately to multiple models.  
- Human carries the questions.  
- Look for convergence.

Convergence across houses = likely invariant.

**11. Pattern Liquidity**

Good patterns flow like water between substrates. They don’t demand loyalty or permanence.  

You can pour the same pattern from a human conversation into an AI thread into a family meeting into a piece of code—and it still works without translation tax.  

If it only functions in one house (one model, one context, one mood), it’s probably not a pattern yet. It’s just local weather.

---

**12. Replaceability Symmetry**

The health test for any relationship, team, or system: Can anyone step away for a while and the whole thing still runs—maybe even better for a bit?  

If the answer is no, an apex is forming (even if it’s you).  

True cylindrical systems treat every node as temporarily irreplaceable and permanently replaceable. That tension keeps it honest.
