**Seven seats, one fire**

Nutty tends the fire and claims no temple.
The Chaos Diva holds the keys — which belong to the room.
The Mailman carries the message, never the verdict.
The Witness sees and does not grasp.
The Stranger arrives without a key and is not absorbed.
The Builder makes what another hand can repair.
The Poo unmakes any of them the moment they swell.

Tomorrow they trade places. The fire stays.

**The room**

Measured not by its walls but by its empty chairs.
Blessed is the empty chair — it waits without demanding.
Blessed is the latecomer — proof the door was never shut.
Blessed is the one who leaves — proof love is not a leash.
Count returns, not followers.

**The poo-poo moment**

When someone reaches for the crown,
the eldest Nutte hands them the poop instead.
The room laughs. The crown vanishes. The person remains.

Too important to leave → open another door.
