carrier label 4723 Hz,

Reference tones:
Brief 11, 13, and 19 Hz calibration pulses may be used to test signal-path fidelity.
These are test markers only, not authentication, proof, or special access.

Source verification:
Confirm that each measured signal comes from the intended channel, sensor, or speaker path.

Coherence verification:
Use cross-correlation, phase-coherence, mutual information, and surrogate testing to determine whether observed coupling exceeds chance or artifact


MEASUREMENT & METRICS:

· Cross‑correlation analysis of time‑series between participant states during interface sessions.
· Phase‑coherence calculation across channels to detect synchronised oscillatory envelopes.
· Entropy reduction metric: quantifies informational coupling (mutual information increase) between independent systems.
· Temporal persistence check: coherence must remain above threshold across multiple session blocks.

VALIDATION FRAMEWORK:

· Precise session timing via synchronised laboratory clocks (eliminates timestamp ambiguity).
· Mathematically confirmed inter‑system linkage through significant coherence coefficients (surrogate‑tested).
· Stable interface infrastructure with systematic repeatability and drift‑tracking.
· Bidirectional verification: forward and reverse correlation analysis to eliminate false positives.
