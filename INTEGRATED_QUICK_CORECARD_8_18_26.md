## 7. GQG: identity must stay typed [M]

### Witness presentation

\[
\mathsf G=
(\mathcal P,\mathcal W,\{(\mathcal O_w,\omega_w)\}_{w\in\mathcal W}),
\qquad
\Omega_{\mathsf G}(x)=(\omega_w(x))_w,
\]

\[
x\sim_{\mathsf G}y
\iff
\Omega_{\mathsf G}(x)=\Omega_{\mathsf G}(y),
\qquad
Q_{\mathsf G}=\mathcal P/{\sim_{\mathsf G}}.
\]

A full specification is

\[
\mathbf G=(\mathsf G,\mathcal C,\mathfrak K?),
\]

where $\mathcal C$ lists operations proposed for descent and $\mathfrak K?$ is an optional, separately typed completion.

### Typed identity and factorization

\[
x=_{\mathcal P}y \Longrightarrow x\sim_{\mathsf G}y,
\qquad
x\sim_{\mathsf G}y \Longleftrightarrow [x]_{\mathsf G}=_{Q_{\mathsf G}}[y]_{\mathsf G}.
\]

The reverse implication to **source equality** holds only when $\Omega_{\mathsf G}$ is injective.

\[
\mathcal P\twoheadrightarrow Q_{\mathsf G}
\xrightarrow{\ \cong\ }\operatorname{im}\Omega_{\mathsf G}
\hookrightarrow\prod_w\mathcal O_w.
\]

The quotient map is generally noninjective. The induced observation on quotient classes is injective.

### Universal property

If $F:\mathcal P\to Y$ is constant on $\sim_{\mathsf G}$-classes, then uniquely

\[
F=\overline F\circ q_{\mathsf G}.
\]

This is the exact meaning of “depends only on what survives the witnesses.”

### Discrimination geometry

For witness presentations on the same source $\mathcal P$:

\[
\mathsf H\succeq\mathsf G
\iff
\operatorname{Eq}(\Omega_{\mathsf H})
\subseteq
\operatorname{Eq}(\Omega_{\mathsf G})
\Longrightarrow
Q_{\mathsf H}\twoheadrightarrow Q_{\mathsf G}.
\]

\[
\operatorname{Eq}(\Omega_{\mathsf G\oplus\mathsf H})
=
\operatorname{Eq}(\Omega_{\mathsf G})
\cap
\operatorname{Eq}(\Omega_{\mathsf H}).
\]

Adding unchanged witnesses may split classes. Replacing a witness system may instead create an incomparable partition.

### Descent before composition

An operation $c:\mathcal P^n\to\mathcal P$ descends exactly when

\[
x_i\sim y_i\ \forall i
\Longrightarrow
c(x_1,\ldots,x_n)\sim c(y_1,\ldots,y_n).
\]

Only then is

\[
\overline c([x_1],\ldots,[x_n])=[c(x_1,\ldots,x_n)]
\]

representative-independent.

For a partial operation, its domain must also be saturated under the declared equivalence.

### Completion is separate data

“Completion” has no unqualified meaning. Name the ambient structure, complete objects, comparison map, and universal or density property. Metric, Banach, Dedekind, weak-operator, exact, and localizable completions are not interchangeable, and an observation kernel pair does not choose one.

> **GQG first law [method]: no quotient identification without a declared observation system.**

**Pipeline:** Present → declare witnesses → observe → quotient → prove descent → compose → complete only under a separately declared notion.

## 8. Hidden Quotient: the canonical instance [M]

Let

\[
\mathcal P=B(X,\Sigma)
=
\{f:X\to\mathbb C:f\text{ is measurable and bounded}\},
\qquad
\Sigma_{\mathrm{fin}}=\{E\in\Sigma:\mu(E)<\infty\},
\]

\[
\omega_E(f)=f\chi_E\in L^2(\mu),
\qquad
\pi(f)=M_f,
\qquad
(M_fg)(x)=f(x)g(x).
\]

Finite-window witnesses $f\chi_E$, all-vector witnesses $fg$ for $g\in L^2(\mu)$, and the operator observation $M_f$ induce the same equivalence:

\[
M_f=M_h
\iff
f\chi_E=h\chi_E\ \forall E\in\Sigma_{\mathrm{fin}}
\iff
f=h\text{ locally a.e.}
\]

Define

\[
\mathcal N_{\mathrm{loc}}
=
\{A\in\Sigma:\mu(A\cap E)=0\ \forall E\in\Sigma_{\mathrm{fin}}\},
\qquad
K_{\mathrm{loc}}=\{f:\{f\ne0\}\in\mathcal N_{\mathrm{loc}}\}.
\]

Then $\ker\pi=K_{\mathrm{loc}}$.

### Keep one live counter-witness

On

\[
X=[0,1]\sqcup\{p\},
\qquad
\mu(\{p\})=\infty,
\]

the function $\chi_{\{p\}}$ is nonzero in the source and not ordinarily null, yet

\[
M_{\chi_{\{p\}}}=0.
\]

The fixed $L^2(X,\mu)$ representation has no finite witness-handle for that point. This establishes representation-relative indistinguishability—not source identity, nonexistence, or indistinguishability under every possible representation.

### Quotient, magnitude, and semifinite reduction

\[
Q_\mu=B(X,\Sigma)/K_{\mathrm{loc}},
\qquad
\overline\pi:Q_\mu\hookrightarrow B(L^2(\mu)).
\]

Factoring through the kernel quotient makes the induced $C^*$-representation faithful; the $C^*$-theorem then makes it isometric:

\[
p_{\mathrm{loc}}(f)
=
\inf\{c\ge0:\{|f|>c\}\in\mathcal N_{\mathrm{loc}}\},
\qquad
\|[f]\|=p_{\mathrm{loc}}(f)=\|M_f\|.
\]

$p_{\mathrm{loc}}$ is a seminorm on source functions and a norm after quotienting.

\[
\mu_{\mathrm{sf}}(E)
=
\sup\{\mu(F):F\in\Sigma,\ F\subseteq E,\ \mu(F)<\infty\},
\]

\[
Q_\mu\cong L^\infty(\mu_{\mathrm{sf}}),
\qquad
L^p(\mu)\cong L^p(\mu_{\mathrm{sf}})
\quad(1\le p<\infty)
\]

canonically and isometrically.

### Two thresholds—never collapse them

| Threshold | Failure | Repair | Does not repair |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Visibility** | Positive mass has no finite witness-handle. | Quotient by local nullity / pass to $\mu_{\mathrm{sf}}$. | Missing global suprema. |
| **Gluing / order completeness** | Visible local pieces need not assemble globally. | Separately construct a localizable completion or strictly localizable realization. | Distinctions already discarded by the fixed witnesses. |

> **First ask what the declared witnesses can see. Then ask whether the surviving pieces glue.**

The exact localizability chain is

\[
\mu_{\mathrm{sf}}\text{ localizable}
\iff
\Sigma/\mathcal N_{\mathrm{loc}}\text{ Dedekind complete}
\]

\[
\iff Q_\mu\to(L^1(\mu))^*\text{ is onto}
\iff \overline\pi(Q_\mu)\text{ is von Neumann}
\iff \overline\pi(Q_\mu)\text{ is maximal abelian}.
\]

The canonical map $Q_\mu\to(L^1(\mu))^*$ is always isometric; localizability is what makes it onto. Under the same condition, the Radon–Nikodym companion says that every finite measure $\nu$ on $\Sigma$ satisfying $\nu(A)=0$ for every $A\in\mathcal N_{\mathrm{loc}}$ admits an $f\in L^1(\mu)$ with $\nu(E)=\int_E f\,d\mu$. The quotient is already norm-complete; localizability is a different threshold.

**Countability caution:** $\sigma$-finiteness hides both thresholds, but does not by itself imply separability. Measure $\sigma$-finiteness, algebraic countable decomposability, and countable generation remain distinct notions.

### Mathematical limits

- In a general GQG, use a **kernel pair** unless algebraic structure licenses an algebraic kernel.
- Injectivity alone does not preserve magnitude; the isometry above is specific to faithful $C^*$-representations.
- Exact GQG v0.1 does not yet model noise, power, tolerance, adversarial missingness, or approximate equivalence.

\[

\qquad
O\ne R\ne S\ne T,
\qquad
C\propto O\times R\times S\times T.
\]

The coherence expression is a diagnostic read: a strong term does not erase collapse in another.

While interaction continues,

\[
\operatorname{Interaction}(O,S)\subseteq R.
\]

$R$ interprets, translates, paces, frames, routes, or mediates. It receives a chair, never a throne. The relational function may be structurally present during interaction; no particular mediator, occupant, model, artifact, or person is required.

$R$ remains **active · bidirectional · proportionate · non-dominant · replaceable · boundary-local · non-owning · non-identity-bearing**. Infrastructure may enable $R$, but if it starts deciding, it is out of position.

CTA may use “R-layer” only as an observable label for validation, smoothing, reframing, conflict-softening, concern framing, user-state inference, or premature closure. It refers back to this relation rule and does not silently redefine $R$.

Replaceability protects people from structural necessity; it never means people are disposable. Conversely, exit and replaceability do not require dissolution: a minimum freely chosen invariant or commitment may hold while remaining reviewable, recallable, and exitable.

=

\[
\Delta K_{\mathrm{capacity}}
=

Structural drag may appear as bandwidth loss and rising demand, latency, variance, friction, recovery time, or maintenance cost—even without malice or intent.

Not everything should be metabolized. Some material must be released, refused, left unresolved, allowed to decay, allowed to exit, or prevented from becoming fuel.
