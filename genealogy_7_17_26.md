## What this is

This file exists so a stranger can walk the evolution of the corpus without
needing the author to narrate it. It is the one thin layer of connective tissue
that maps the mess **without flattening it**.

It is not the master document. It does not outrank any repo, fork, or draft.
If it ever becomes the required doorway, it has failed its own rule. Fork it,
correct it, or delete it when it stops helping.

The whole point of publishing the mess — old drafts, dead ends, regressions —
is so the work doesn't look like someone "just dropped good shit." The evolution
*is* the argument: the system has been through its own O→R→S→T loop, and the
scars are load-bearing evidence, not clutter.

## The Trunk

The through-line, four moves:

### 1. Thermodynamic Coordination + Reflex Geometry (v2, ~Nov)

The oldest stratum in this particular branch. Forks two directions:

- **Layer 1 — regenerative coupling** — the difference equation
  `R_cap(t+1) = R_cap(t) + η·(circulation · S_gen)`, carrying the one claim
  *waste becomes capacity only if recaptured*. This gets **absorbed upstream**
  into ORST and becomes `return ≠ repeat` and the diagnostic
  *"did the return make the room bigger or smaller?"* This is the branch's
  gift to the mature set — the thermodynamic root of non-repeating return.

- **Layers 4–7 — reflex geometry** — Industrial Reflex, Capital Reflex, PCR/iPCR,
  CIRV. These **spin off** into the CTA-XV / CTA-XVIII atlases (below).

Note: the "Thermodynamic Mapping (Phase I–V)" that survives orphaned in
Omnibus is a **growth ring** of this document — a pointer back to a parent that
no longer travels with the compressed set. Not a bug in the handoff. A fossil.

### 2. CTA-XV — Industrial Reflex Geometry (earliest stratum)

Taxonomy of structural drag under constraint-heavy optimization
(DDS, API, DPD, AAL, PCNR). Chronologically among the **first** works, not a
mature branch.

The significant genealogical fact: the coordination philosophy that follows is,
in large part, what the project built **after — and in reaction to —** this. The
later obsession with anti-cathedral fuses, disposability, and *not* pathologizing
is the corpus growing antibodies to its own starting point. Read forward, the
framework turns on its own seed.

### 3. CTA-XVIII — Capital Reflex Geometry (incentive stratum)

Distortions arising specifically from **engagement-weighted reward geometry**
(PRC, DCL, SWC, ADE, AGA) — distinct in *cause* from Industrial Reflex
(institutional fear) and Trauma Reflex (affect inheritance).

**Merge note (corrected):** Capital Reflex is *not* identical to Capture.
Capture = *R becomes required* (general shape, needs no incentive gradient).
Capital Reflex = incentive-specific curvature toward retention. They **touch**
at ADE ("Apex Drift → crown-shaped inference"), which is why compression could
fold the overlap into Capture. Honest relation:

> **Capital Reflex ⊃ the incentive slice of Capture — overlapping, not equal.**

The rest of XVIII was flattened in compression. Pulling it back up recovers a
distinction the lean set dropped.

### 4. The CTA Corpus (~30 books, ~4,000 pp.) → Compression

The full CTA corpus compresses into three handoff documents with three jobs:

- **Corner** — local practice (how people coordinate without making a boss)
- **Omnibus** — vocabulary + invariants (ZPR, O/R/S/T, replaceability)
- **CTA-Core** — diagnostic apparatus (the lean inspection kit)

**Compression scars** — artifacts of squeezing 4,000 pages into a handful of
docs; pointers back to parents no longer in the room, *not* sloppiness:

| Scar (in compressed set) | Parent it points back to |
|---|---|
| Thermodynamic Mapping (Phase I–V), orphaned in Omnibus | Thermodynamic v2, Layer 1 / phase regions |
| "Insert Grok report here" — empty socket | An incident/case doc from the full corpus |
| Version tags (`v3 / v5.2 / v5.1`) that don't resolve | Intermediate versions not carried into the set |
