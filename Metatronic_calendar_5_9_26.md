Variation Rule: The order of the tree does not vary. The playable sequence remains O → R → S → T → S′ → R′ → O₂. Variation occurs through octave, duration, emphasis, texture, and harmonic transposition, not through rearranging the tree.
Second Series Rule: Months 8–13 are return-transpositions of Months 1–6. They reuse the first series as transformed echoes rather than introducing unrelated tones.
Calendar Anchor: Day 1 begins at the first sunrise after the spring equinox. The 365th day remains outside the count as the Unowned Day / Day of the Serpent.
Edge Rule: 109/211 Hz is a threshold pair. 109 Hz marks the lower/body edge; 211 Hz marks the upper/signal edge. It may be played sequentially, layered as a drone, or divided between low and high instruments.
