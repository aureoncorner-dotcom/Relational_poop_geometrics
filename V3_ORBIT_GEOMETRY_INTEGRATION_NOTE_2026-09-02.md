## Decision

The toroidal packet improves this project as a **discipline of distinctions**, not as a new explanatory mythology.

| Question | Plain-language form | What it can establish | What it cannot establish |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Quotient / representation** | Which differences did this record, score, summary, or category erase? | A declared representation loses specified distinctions. | Why the source differed, who intended the loss, or what will happen next. |
| **Orbit / accessibility** | Which routes or states are actually reachable, rather than merely advertised? | Formal route availability differs from observed usable access. | That every available route is fair, affordable, independent, or effective. |
| **Cocycle / carried history** | What change, authorization, or transformation accumulated along the route? | A declared, retained history can be audited across steps. | That a repeated pattern has one cause or carries moral guilt. |
| **Closure / sufficiency** | Does the retained state contain enough context to determine the next declared update? | A bounded model or procedure passes, fails, or has not yet received a closure test. | The truth of a larger causal theory, hidden controller, or social diagnosis. |

This is a stronger form of the manuscript's existing rule: **a map is not the territory, and a record is not the person.**

## Editorial boundary

Use the geometry only where it changes an audit, a record, or a test.

Do not use it as evidence that myth, Revelation, an institution, a platform, or a person is literally toroidal, governed by a cocycle, or controlled by a hidden quotient. In historical chapters, geometry may supply a visibly marked **symbolic** metaphor only. It has no evidentiary standing there.

A cross-document relation must retain its type:

- `EXACT_QUOTIENT` only for a declared map with a proved quotient identity;
- `DRIVEN_COCYCLE` only when increments, retained driver, and concatenation law are specified;
- `AUGMENTED_STATE_CLOSURE` only after an explicit sufficient-state update is constructed and tested;
- `FORMAL_PROPOSAL_ONLY` for a preregistered but unrun test; and
- `EMPIRICAL_INSTANTIATION` only with independent observation and validation.

Similarity, shared vocabulary, and a useful analogy are not descent certificates.

## Reader-facing grafts for a future v3

### Chapter 6 — Silicon God Necropolis

Add after the operational-quotient discussion:

> A compact record is not automatically a sufficient record. Two people may receive the same score, category, or generated summary while differing in information necessary to the next decision. Before an institution treats equal outputs as equal cases, it must identify what the representation forgot, retain a route to the underlying evidence, and permit a counterexample to reopen the classification.

This makes the critique of profiles and synthetic likeness more exact. It does not make every summary abusive; it makes consequential summaries contestable.

### Chapter 7 — Who Owns “Next”?

Add to the access-clock discussion:

> An announced appeal, portability mechanism, or alternative provider is not yet a usable route. The audit asks who can reach it, how long it takes, what it costs, what information it requires, whether retaliation is possible, and whether use of the route changes the decision. A route that exists only in policy is not plural access.

This translates the formal/observed accessibility distinction without pretending civic life is a lattice simulation.

### Chapter 8 — practical constitution

Add a thirteenth test:

13. **No forced closure.** When a score, summary, policy, or model treats cases as equivalent, can a person identify the missing context, reach the underlying record, and obtain meaningful reconsideration? A compact view may guide action; it may not silently erase the distinction needed to challenge that action.

This joins—rather than replaces—person before representation, plural route, and immutable witness.

### Appendix A — evidence discipline

For every consequential pattern, prediction, or simulation claim, record separately:

- source state and declared representation or projection;
- formal possibility versus observed accessibility or mixing;
- retained transformation history, version, and correction path;
- exact next-step or outcome claim being tested;
- eligible counterexample domain; and
- result status: `CLOSED`, `FAILED`, or `UNRESOLVED`.

`UNRESOLVED` is not confirmation. A failed compact model may justify retaining more **observed** context; it does not justify inventing invisible context until the failure disappears.

## Reusable audit card

1. **Representation:** What did the system observe, and what distinctions did its summary erase?
2. **Route:** What paths exist in principle, and which are usable in practice by the affected person?
3. **History:** Which transformations, permissions, corrections, and refusals travelled with the artifact?
4. **Decision:** What rule converted the representation into a consequence?
5. **Closure:** Does the retained state predict the next declared decision or outcome on a predeclared domain?
6. **Counterexample:** If equal outputs lead to different next outcomes, is the pair preserved and does the system name the missing witnessed context?
7. **Remedy:** Does a successful challenge change the record, decision, route, or authorization—not merely the explanation?

The card diagnoses information loss and route failure. It does not diagnose intent, coordination, guilt, or metaphysical status.
