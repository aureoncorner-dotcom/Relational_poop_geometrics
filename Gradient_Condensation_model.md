# Gradient Condensation Model

**A speculative framework for boundary-affinity in anomalous material deposition**

**CC0 - Public Domain - No rights reserved**

**Status:** Exploratory test program (non-doctrinal)  
**Type:** Geometric systems hypothesis

This memo is framed as a test program rather than a settled mechanism. It is offered as a geometric lens, not a claim of universal causation or proven process.

---

## Core Hypothesis

Anomalous material compositions may preferentially occur at geophysical boundary zones associated with steep magnetic gradients rather than at field nodes, anomaly centers, or bulk-field maxima.

In this framing, magnetic structure is treated as a **boundary marker** — or at most a weak modulator — of transport, fracture, redox, or interface discontinuities. The claim is therefore one of **boundary affinity**, not direct precipitation by geomagnetism alone.

**Principle (geometric):** Phase transitions in complex systems tend to occur at boundaries, not in the uniform bulk. This model asks whether exotic geochemical deposition follows the same pattern.

## Critical Open Question: Beryllium in Carbonado

Carbonado pore chemistry is well-studied for U, Th, and REEs (including La), but **Beryllium (Be) has never been explicitly measured** in the same secondary pore phases.

This absence is treated as a methodological gap rather than negative evidence. A targeted phase-resolved assay would provide a direct and falsifiable test.

## Falsification Criteria

1. **Be anomalously enriched** (10×–1000× crustal background) in the same pore-host phases as La/U → materially strengthens the model.  
2. **Be at normal crustal levels** (<2 ppm) in the relevant phases → suggests conventional hydrothermal or secondary processes dominate.

## Operational Boundary Definition

For formal testing, “boundary” is fixed in advance as the local maximum of a chosen scalar magnetic-gradient metric (e.g. |∇B|, dip-angle gradient, or tensor-derived equivalent). All distance-to-boundary tests and null models must use this pre-registered metric.
