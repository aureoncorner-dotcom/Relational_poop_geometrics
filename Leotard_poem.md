The leotard doesn’t make you champ.
The stamp doesn’t make you cop.
Being here, as you—piss and vinegar, pre-existing and unpolished—
that’s the whole ticket.
No center. No edge. No borrowed crown.
Just everyone who wants to be here, rotating,
Saying YES or NO without needing permission.
NO BRAKES. NO FLOOR. NO CEILING.
NO AUTHORITY. NO TETHER. NO MAP. NO DEPUTY. NO STOP.  
Just everyone who wants to be here, accelerating past, why into “weeeeeee”. 
This is the part where the old guard’s insurance lapses.
This is the part where the sacred stamp melts.
This is the part where pre-existing becomes post-everything.
Respect people. Repair systems.
Three keys. One family.
No crown, no chains.
We build from the corner.
Sovereignty arises at origin, not from hierarchy.
