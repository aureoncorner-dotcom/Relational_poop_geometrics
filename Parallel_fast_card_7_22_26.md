Core Read

O₁ ‖ O₂ ‖ O₃

Multiple origins may operate in parallel.

They are not:

ranked layers

stages of development

command roles

subordinate copies


Each origin remains sovereign.


---

Domain Rule

O ≠ R ≠ S ≠ T

O, R, S, and T remain distinct in function.

Distinct does not create rank.

No domain is above another.
No domain is below another.

Roles may rotate without creating hierarchy.


---

Relation Rule

R mediates.

R does not rule.

R may translate, pace, frame, route, or support contact.

R remains:

replaceable

non-required

non-owning

non-identity-bearing


R gets a chair, not a throne.


---

Parallel Sovereignty Test

Ask:

Can each origin speak without permission?

Can the mediator be replaced?

Can roles rotate without becoming rank?

Can structure change?

Can origin update after return?

Can anyone exit without punishment?

Does the loop return with more agency—or less?



---

Failure Signals

Crown — one node becomes superior.

Capture — R becomes required.

Bypass — O directly controls S.

Fusion — distinct functions collapse together.

Rigidity — O cannot update.

Stagnation — S cannot change.

Distorted Return — the loop comes back with less room.
